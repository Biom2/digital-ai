What we're here to do

The role of the central Digital & AI team is to collate, prioritize, maintain and drive delivery of Digital & AI use cases across all GOFSW&UK functions to deliver measurable ROI, providing targeted data engineering and development capabilities as required.

03 / 07
How it works

From idea to delivery

01

Longlist

Anyone in the business can submit a use case. No filter at this stage — if it takes time or creates risk, it's worth capturing.

02

Score

Each idea is scored on Impact (1–5) and Feasibility (1–5). The product places it in one of four quadrants.

03

Prioritise

Quadrant A (high impact, high feasibility) goes to the top of the backlog. The rest is planned, monitored, or parked.

04

Deliver

We build with the relevant function, validate the output, and measure the result against the original ROI estimate.

Impact × Feasibility
Transparent, revisable, backlog-driven
Impact — time saved, revenue, risk, compliance1 – 5
Feasibility — data availability, complexity, build time1 – 5

The 3-check rule

Before sharing any AI output — is it accurate? Could it contain sensitive data? Would you put your name on it?

Why this matters
⚠️
Risk
Sensitive data entered into a public AI tool can be used to train models or exposed externally. It's happened to other organisations — including in our sector.
📋
Compliance
Using unapproved tools may breach our data governance policy, GDPR obligations, and contractual confidentiality clauses.
🏢
Reputation
An AI-generated output shared externally without review reflects on Masdar UK — not just the individual who sent it.

What we need from you

Share your use cases

If something takes more than 1 hour a week or involves unnecessary manual steps => that's a use case. It doesn't need to be a fully formed idea. Message Mohamed on Teams or flag it today.

Flag tools & software

If your team is evaluating a vendor or already using a tool informally — loop us in. We can assess it, avoid duplication, and make sure it fits the architecture.

Become an AI Champion

We're looking for one person per team willing to be the link between their colleagues and what we're building. No technical background needed — just curiosity and a willingness to flag what's not working. What it means in practice: share ideas, give early feedback on tools before they go live, and help your team use AI safely. About an hour a month
